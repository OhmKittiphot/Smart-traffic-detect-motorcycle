{# Speed calibration editor (calibration.js): click the 4 points on a frame and enter the real size #}

Speed calibration

Click 4 points on the road that form a rectangle, in order: 1 far-left, 2 far-right, 3 near-right, 4 near-left. Drag a point to move it. Then enter the real size of that rectangle on the ground (measure on site, or use known markings such as the lane width).
